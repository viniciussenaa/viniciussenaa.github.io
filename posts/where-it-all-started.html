Where it all started

19/07/2026 · about me

My first contact with Linux happened when my dad came home with an old beige PC — CRT monitor, an Ubuntu 5.04 CD, and a printed magazine that walked you through the installation step by step. He told me it didn't get viruses and it was free. I followed the guide, got it running, and that was it. I was hooked.

That was my introduction to Linux and to the open source culture around it. I didn't fully understand what I was doing at the time, but something clicked.

Fast forward: I've spent the last 7 years working in NOC — monitoring infrastructure, responding to incidents, keeping things running at 3am when they shouldn't be breaking. It gave me a solid foundation, but I kept feeling pulled toward something more hands-on. Building pipelines. Automating the boring parts. Owning reliability end to end.

That's what I want to do next. And this blog is where I'll document the path — DevOps, Linux, networking, security, and everything in between.

If you're on a similar track, stick around.
